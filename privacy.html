TankGo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What we collect

TankGo does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases

Optional Premium purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. TankGo never receives your name, e-mail address or payment details; the app only learns from Google Play whether Premium is active, and stores that on your device.

What stays on your device

Your chosen language, country and theme, and the last downloaded price data, are saved only on your device so the app works offline. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network requests

The app downloads fuel price files from GitHub Pages (nashwanadil.github.io). As with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or log this information.

Location

The optional "nearby stations" feature asks for your approximate location. It is used only on your device to sort stations by distance. TankGo downloads the complete station list of a country and filters it locally, so your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored.

Permissions

The app only requests internet access. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or any other data.

Data sources

EU Weekly Oil Bulletin (European Commission), CBS Statistics Netherlands (CC BY 4.0) and the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA). Prices are national averages, may be several days old, and are provided for information only without warranty.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/nashwanadil/fuelprices/issues.
